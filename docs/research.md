# Research and verification

This register separates source documentation, implementation checks and live host acceptance. **Confirmed** means stated by a cited source or directly observed in the named check; **not stated** marks what that evidence does not establish. Requirements belong in [product-spec.md](product-spec.md), and technical contracts belong in [implementation-plan.md](implementation-plan.md).

The historical platform and dataset source baseline was reviewed on 2026-10-17, with later dates stated below. Those dates are not a claim that third-party capabilities were rechecked during the repository cleanup. Production inventory, private installation identifiers and personal investigation records are maintained outside this repository.
